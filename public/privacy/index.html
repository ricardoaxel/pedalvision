Privacy

Pedalvision is a local-first planner. Your boards stay in your browser unless you choose to export or share them.

Last updated 3 November 2026

01

What stays on your device

There is no account and no Pedalvision server storing your boards in this version. Scenes, custom gear, exported files and the scene list are handled in the browser:

  • Scenes are saved in the browser's IndexedDB.
  • Settings, the scene index and first-run state are kept in localStorage.
  • Exports are generated in the browser and downloaded directly.

Clearing this site's data in your browser removes those local records. If you want a portable copy, use the scene backup download or export the board.

02

Share links

A share link contains the scene itself, encoded after the # in the URL. That part of a URL is not sent to the server. Anyone who has the link can open the scene; it is a read-only copy, not a private account document.

Scene names, custom part names and layout data are part of the link. Do not put private information in a scene you share.

03

Analytics and cookies

This build sets no marketing or analytics cookies, and ships no product analytics, session replay or third-party tracking scripts. If analytics is added later, this page and the changelog will be updated first, and consent will be handled before any optional tracking runs.

04

Catalog images and external sites

Catalog images are hosted by Pedalvision and used to identify gear and plan at true scale. The image credits page explains how they are sourced and how to request a change or removal.

Following a GitHub or email link leaves Pedalvision and is governed by that service's own privacy terms.

05

Your choices

  • Delete individual scenes in the Scenes panel.
  • Clear this site's data in your browser to remove local storage and IndexedDB records.
  • Export a scene before clearing data if you want to keep it.
  • Use the image credits and takedown path for catalog requests.
06

Contact and changes

Questions about this page can go to ricardoaxelgd@gmail.com or the project's GitHub issues. When the app's data handling changes, this page and the changelog are updated together.
