Community guidelines

Pedalvision grows through corrections and evidence, not a submission backend. These guidelines keep that work useful and fair.

Last updated 3 November 2026

01

Where contributions happen

This version has no accounts, public profiles or upload queue. Corrections, image removal requests and bug reports go through the project's GitHub issues. Maintainers make the final call on what ships.

02

Be respectful

  • Discuss the gear and the evidence, not the person.
  • No harassment, hate speech, threats, spam or deliberate disruption.
  • Assume a correction may be right; ask for the source before arguing.
  • Keep reports on topic and in English.
03

Corrections and evidence

A useful correction includes:

  • the item, brand and model (or the page URL);
  • the field that is wrong and the value you believe is correct;
  • a verifiable source — official manual, product page, support page or a clear photo;
  • for jack/IO corrections, the connector face and the source that shows it.

"Looks wrong" is not enough to change a dimension, jack or power value. If you cannot verify it, report it as a question instead.

04

Images and rights

  • Only submit images you have the right to share.
  • Prefer official brand/press/support images, then authorized retailers.
  • Do not copy product descriptions in bulk.
  • Record where an image came from; per-item provenance stays in the private catalog record.
  • Removal requests take precedence — use the takedown path.
05

Privacy and security

Do not post personal data about yourself or anyone else in an issue. For a security or privacy problem, email ricardoaxelgd@gmail.com instead of opening a public issue.

06

Moderation

Maintainers may edit, close or remove contributions that break these guidelines or that cannot be supported with sources. Repeated abuse can lead to a block from the project's spaces. Removal requests for catalog material are honoured under the takedown policy.
