Terms of use

Plain-language terms for using Pedalvision. It is a planning aid, not a substitute for the manufacturer's specifications.

Last updated 3 November 2026

01

The planner

Pedalvision is an independent, local-first pedalboard planner. You do not need an account to use it. Scenes are stored in your browser and share links contain the scene in the URL; see the privacy page for the data details.

02

A planning aid, not a spec sheet

Catalogue dimensions, jack positions, power details and images are provided to help you plan. They may be incomplete, approximate or out of date. Always verify the physical footprint, power requirements, connectors and safety information against the manufacturer's current documentation before you buy, drill, wire or power anything.

You are responsible for your own build, including clearances, cable lengths, power polarity and load.

Images are used for identification and true-scale planning; the image credits and takedown page explains the source policy and removal path.

The app is provided as-is, without warranties of any kind. Features may change, and local storage can be cleared by your browser or device. We do not guarantee that a scene, share link or export will always be available. Export or back up work you want to keep.
