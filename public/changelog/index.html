Changelog

One entry per shipped feature, newest first. Each feature PR updates the top of this list and the feature catalogue.

  1. 10 Nov 2026

    Similar pedals Added

    Select a placed catalogue pedal and its info panel now suggests up to four alternatives that share an effect type. Tap one to open the pedal picker already filtered to that effect, so you can compare and place without retyping a search. Nothing in your scene changes until you choose to add a pedal; custom parts and read-only share views do not show the section.

  2. 7 Nov 2026

    Pedal labels Added

    Give any placed pedal, supply or custom part a short personal label — “always on”, “Klon clone”, “dirt 1” — from the Label field in its info panel. The note appears as a second line under the name chip on the canvas and in the panel, so a rig reads the way you think about it. Labels save, travel in share links and reproduce in PNG/PDF. Clearing the field removes the note; the catalogue name is never changed.

  3. 6 Nov 2026

    Organization colors Added

    Give any placed pedal, supply or board a custom color so a rig reads at a glance — mark your drives, power section or modulation row without recoloring the artwork. A selected part gets a slim color outline; a selected board gets a colored border while its finish stays as chosen. Use Color in the action bar for five fixed inks, a custom color from your device's picker, or Default to clear it. Colors save, travel in share links and reproduce in PNG/PDF.

  4. 5 Nov 2026

    Find stereo-capable pedals Added

    The pedal picker now has a Stereo jack filter. Turn it on to see only pedals whose catalogue spec records stereo capability, and combine it with the brand, effect and search filters. Pedals without a recorded stereo spec are not assumed to be mono — they are simply not shown while the filter is on, and the empty state says so.

  5. 5 Nov 2026

    Patch ink for cables Added

    Select a cable in the patch view to give it one of five fixed colors — brick red, orange, green, blue or violet — a custom color from your device's color picker, or the theme's default ink. Colors survive save/load and share links and reproduce in PNG/PDF export. On phones the bar is a compact current-ink control that opens the palette on demand. The fixed inks are calibrated for the dark canvas, the light canvas and white paper; a custom ink that is hard to see gets a small warning.

  6. 4 Nov 2026

    Clearer mobile add Improved

    On phones, the Pedals and Boards tabs in the bottom dock now carry a small +, so adding gear stays obvious after the first-run note is gone. The scene plate also keeps a clean gap from the toolbar on narrow screens instead of being covered.

  7. 3 Nov 2026

    Printable export scale Improved

    PDF export now defaults to a print-ready Fit A4 page (Fit Letter for inch users) at a stated scale such as 1:2.3, with a ruler and a "print at 100%" reference. True size still gives the exact-cm page for plotters and templates, with a warning when it is larger than A4/Letter. PNG keeps its ruler and now carries its intended PPI.

  8. 3 Nov 2026

    Rotation halo fix Fixed

    The selection outline now follows the pedal while it is being rotated, instead of lagging behind and snapping on release.

  9. 3 Nov 2026

    Documentation & legal hub Added

    Public docs, help, feature catalogue, changelog, privacy, terms, community guidelines and image credits/takedown, linked from the planner, the picker and the info panel.

  10. 3 Nov 2026

    Local jack & IO correction Added

    Press Proof lets you drag a placed pedal's jacks to their real position, per scene, including top-face jacks. Share links move to format v3 so corrected anchors travel with the patch.

  11. 31 Oct 2026

    Picker search & filter overhaul Changed

    Punctuation and model aliases, effect-aware search, brand aliases, deterministic sort and a query-echoing empty state.

  12. 30 Oct 2026

    Versioned scene & share persistence Changed

    A supported-version policy and migration chain for saved scenes and shared links; newer files are preserved rather than destroyed.

  13. 30 Oct 2026

    Continuity Check Added

    A derived signal-flow report warns about direction, missing jacks, dangling ends, unconnected chains and loops without blocking edits.

  14. 29 Oct 2026

    Motion system Added

    One restrained motion language for panels, item changes and scene switches; reduced-motion is instant.

  15. 28 Oct 2026

    Viewport focus Added

    Frame the selected board or used area, page through boards and lock the camera while editing; every scene load frames itself.

  16. 28 Oct 2026

    Margin Rule measurement HUD Added

    Visible extent and grid readout, a true-length reference, a selectable grid step and a temporary measure tool.

  17. 27 Oct 2026

    Thumbnail renditions Changed

    Small picker rows and the mobile info sleeve use the 350 px image rendition; detailed surfaces keep the 800 px master.

  18. 27 Oct 2026

    Read-only jack ticks & cable-aware export Changed

    Shared previews show real jack sockets, and selected-board exports follow the exact cable curve so long handles are not clipped.
