Catalog image credits & removal

Pedalvision shows product images only to help you identify gear and plan a pedalboard at true scale. Brand and product names are factual references; Pedalvision is an independent planner and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the brands shown.

Last updated 3 November 2026

01

How images are used

  • Images are hosted by Pedalvision and used for identification and true-scale planning only.
  • We do not copy product descriptions or imply brand endorsement.
  • Per-item source records are kept internally for provenance and improvement. Some legacy images are marked for replacement.
02

Source policy

Pedal images follow this order: official brand/press/support, then an authorized retailer, then a recorded user/community image, then a recorded planner image as a last-resort shipped source, then our own generated flat art. Board images use official/authorized references for proportions and color, but the shipped pixels are our own generated flat art.

Per-item source URLs stay in the private catalog record and are never rendered on this page. That record is what lets future work replace weaker sources.

03

Removal request

If an image or fact is yours and you would like it credited differently, replaced or removed, contact us and we will act on it. No legal threat is required.

Email

ricardoaxelgd@gmail.com

Or open an issue

GitHub issue

Please include the catalog item (or page URL) and the requested action: remove, replace or credit.

04

What happens next

Removal requests take precedence and are honoured promptly. We acknowledge the request, remove or replace the asset in the catalog, and record the change in the item's provenance. The affected asset is pulled even if no replacement is ready.
