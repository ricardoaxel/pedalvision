01 / The opportunity

Product vision

North star

Build an independent pedal encyclopedia and discovery engine—not just another list of product pages. Make it easier for musicians to find the right sound, compare options, and explore the long tail of boutique, vintage, discontinued, and experimental effects.

▤

Comprehensive catalog

Search by brand, model, effect type, features, release year, and product status.

⌕

Sound-first discovery

Explore pedals by sound, functionality, musical style, or similarity—not only by manufacturer.

⇄

Useful comparisons

Compare specifications, discover alternatives, and understand how related models differ.

Product principleThe differentiator should be trustworthy information and useful discovery—not merely the largest number of entries.

03 / Rights management

Images need their own workflow

High priority

A public image URL or downloadable press kit is not automatically permission to republish it on a monetized directory.

1

Official brand asset + permission

Ask whether use is permitted on an independent directory that may earn revenue, including thumbnails, resizing, cropping, and future products.

↓
2

Appropriately licensed image

Verify commercial-use, attribution, and modification conditions for the specific image.

↓
3

Ask the builder or use a placeholder

If rights are unclear, request permission and keep the listing image-free until approved.

Track every asset

FieldPurpose
original_source_urlWhere the image came from.
rights_holderManufacturer, photographer, or other owner.
license_typeLicense or permission basis.
commercial_use_allowedWhether the intended use is covered.
modifications_allowedWhether cropping or resizing is permitted.
attribution_requiredRequired credit and placement.
permission_evidenceLicense URL, written approval, or agreement.
statusPending, approved, restricted, or rejected.
Publish only approved assetsDon't hotlink or copy images from other catalogs without permission. Keep a process for replacing assets if a license changes or a complaint arrives.

06 / Automation

AI-assisted ingestion pipeline

Automated steps

  • Discover products from permitted sources.
  • Extract permitted factual fields.
  • Normalize names, aliases, units, and categories.
  • Find likely duplicates while preserving variants.
  • Draft original summaries from verified facts.
  • Monitor stale links and missing data.

Human review gates

  • Check conflicting specifications and dates.
  • Review low-confidence or incomplete records.
  • Verify against manuals or official pages.
  • Approve image permissions separately.
  • Do not let AI invent missing facts.
  • Publish only when required checks pass.

For each field, retain the source, last verification date, and review/confidence status. AI confidence is not proof that a claim is true.

08 / Risk management

Other concerns to plan for

ConcernPractical safeguard
CopyrightUse approved images and original prose; retain permission evidence.
Scraping / site termsCheck terms and prefer APIs, licensed exports, or permission. Distributed or slower scraping does not automatically make it allowed.
Database rightsAvoid bulk or systematic extraction without a clear legal basis; assess jurisdiction-specific rules.
TrademarksUse names accurately, identify the directory as independent, and avoid implying endorsement.
SEO / scaled AI pagesAvoid thin, near-duplicate pages; offer original comparisons and meaningful features.
Affiliate disclosuresClearly disclose commissions and sponsorships; distinguish paid placement from independent rankings.
User uploadsDefine submission rights and provide reporting, moderation, and removal workflows.
PrivacyReview obligations if adding accounts, analytics, newsletters, or saved pedalboards.
Stale dataTrack verification dates, discontinued status, and broken links.

This is practical product guidance, not legal advice. Rules vary by jurisdiction. Before a large commercial import, seek legal advice if a third-party database is central to the product.

09 / Business model

Monetize in stages

↗
Phase A

Affiliate links

Link to relevant retailers or manufacturers. Follow program rules and disclose commissions.

◉
Phase B

Ads & sponsorships

Test advertising when traffic supports it. Clearly label paid placement.

♧
Phase C

Community & premium

Explore saved boards, advanced comparisons, or premium tools after validating demand.

Separate the permissionsAn affiliate relationship does not automatically grant image rights. Check affiliate terms and image permissions independently.
